Route traffic through another country

Pick a country below, connect a supported proxy (Tor, VPN, or SSH tunnel), and all your browser traffic will be encrypted & routed through that country's servers. Sites will see that country's IP address instead of yours.

How to route traffic through another country
  1. Tor Browser — free, encrypted, easiest
    • Download from torproject.org and run it.
    • Pick a country above — the extension fills in geo + timezone.
    • Select "Tor Browser" as the proxy service, or set SOCKS5 127.0.0.1:9150.
    • Click Connect & test. Traffic is encrypted through Tor's 3-hop relay network.
    • Want a specific exit country? Edit Tor's torrc and add ExitNodes {us} (replace us with the 2-letter country code). Tor Browser's torrc is at Browser/TorBrowser/Data/Tor/torrc.
  2. VPN with SOCKS5 — paid, fast, specific countries
    • Mullvad: 10.64.0.1:1080 (connect WireGuard first, then pick any country).
    • ProtonVPN, IVPN, AirVPN: check the dashboard for the SOCKS5 host:port per country server.
    • NordVPN: SOCKS5 available on some plans.
    • Connect the VPN to the country you picked above, then enter the SOCKS5 address here and click Connect & test.
  3. SSH tunnel — your own server
    • ssh -D 1080 -N user@yourserver
    • Set SOCKS5 127.0.0.1:1080. Your traffic exits from your server's country.
  4. Troubleshooting
    • "Proxy unreachable" = the proxy app isn't running. Launch Tor Browser or connect your VPN first.
    • Wrong port — Tor Browser is 9150, Tor daemon is 9050.
    • Stuck with no internet? Click Disconnect or toggle the master switch off. The extension also auto-rollbacks on test failure.

This extension does NOT ship a built-in proxy because bundling a free proxy would just be lying about where your traffic goes. Bring your own trusted one.
